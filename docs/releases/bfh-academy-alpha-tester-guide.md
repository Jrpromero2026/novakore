# Welcome to the Built For Her Academy — Alpha

Thank you for helping us test the Academy. This takes about **20–30 minutes**
across a few sittings. There are no wrong answers — we want your honest
reactions.

## Getting in

1. Open the link we sent you and sign in with your provided details.
2. You'll land on your **Academy home**.

If anything looks off before you even start, that's useful — tell us (below).

## What to do

1. **Start learning.** Tap **Continue learning** to open your Journey,
   _Strong Foundations_.
2. **Work through it.** Read the lessons and try the interactive bits —
   flip the flashcards, answer the quick checks, sit with a reflection.
3. **Take the check-ins.** When a short Evaluation appears, complete it.
4. **Finish a lesson.** Mark it complete and notice how it feels.
5. **Earn your credential.** After the final check, your credential appears on
   your home — open it to see the verify page.
6. **Explore.** Move between the five Programs however you like.

## Tell us what you think — early and often

Use the **Feedback** button (bottom-right, on every screen) any time. Pick a
type and type a sentence. We automatically capture which page you were on, so
you don't need to explain where you were.

Great things to flag:

- Anything confusing, slow, or surprising.
- A lesson that felt too long, too short, or unclear.
- Anything that felt genuinely good or premium.
- Wording that felt off, or a screen that looked broken.

Pick a **severity** if you like: _blocker_ (couldn't continue), _major_
(frustrating), _minor_ (small), or _cosmetic_ (a nit). Not sure? Leave it.

## A few notes

- Your progress saves automatically — leave and come back anytime.
- This is a development version, so a few rough edges are expected. That's
  exactly what you're helping us find.
- Coaches: you'll see the **Coach Certification** track instead of the member
  one — same idea, different content.

## Questions?

Reply to the invite email, or just drop it in the Feedback box. Thank you for
shaping the Academy.
